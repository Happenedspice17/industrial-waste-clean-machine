# Business model

Proposed model; all figures are **estimates** to validate with plant interviews in the next semester. Method:
Business Model Canvas [75].

## Business Model Canvas

| Block | Content |
|---|---|
| **Customer segments** | Machining shops; auto-parts suppliers Tier 1/2; regions Bajío, Estado de México, Nuevo León; buyers: EHS and maintenance managers |
| **Value proposition** | Clean floor at every shift change; oil and chips delivered in two separate streams; no worker contact with fluids; ≈ 1/30 the price of an autonomous scrubber |
| **Channels** | Direct B2B sales; automation distributors; industrial trade fairs |
| **Customer relationships** | Plant pilot before purchase; operator training; service plan with visits |
| **Revenue streams** | Robot ≈ MXN 60,000 (47 % margin); service plan ≈ MXN 1,500/month (battery, brushes, visits); rental option; customer payback ≈ 1.3 years |
| **Key resources** | Robot design and software; engineering team; local supply chain |
| **Key activities** | Design, assembly, firmware; commissioning + route mapping; maintenance and support |
| **Key partners** | Component suppliers in Mexico (UNIT Electronics, Sandorobotics, Steren); authorized hazardous-waste handlers; scrap-metal recyclers; university labs for testing |
| **Cost structure** | Unit ≈ MXN 31,600 (parts, assembly, commissioning); service ≈ MXN 6,800/yr per robot; development ≈ MXN 75,600 one-time |

## Unit economics

| Item | Calculation | Value |
|---|---|--:|
| Unit cost | Hardware 20,160 + assembly 9,000 + commissioning 2,400 | MXN 31,560 |
| Proposed price | Team proposal | MXN 60,000 |
| Gross margin | (60,000 − 31,560) / 60,000 | 47 % |
| Service plan revenue | 1,500 × 12 | MXN 18,000/yr |
| Service cost | Opex of the economic evaluation (battery, consumables, maintenance, electricity) | MXN 6,842/yr |
| Customer gross benefit | Labor saved 38,041 + waste benefit 25,769 | MXN 63,810/yr |
| Customer net benefit with service plan | 63,810 − 18,000 | MXN 45,810/yr |
| Customer payback | 60,000 / 45,810 | ≈ 1.3 years (deduced reconciliation of the presented figure) |
| Price vs commercial scrubber | 60,000 vs ≈ 2,000,000 (Kärcher KIRA B 50) | ≈ 1/30 |

## Value proposition in one sentence

For EHS and maintenance managers of machining plants who need clean floors and segregated waste, the robot cleans
oil and chips at every shift change and hands them over in two separate streams, without water or chemicals and
without workers touching the fluids, for about one thirtieth of the price of an autonomous scrubber that does not
separate waste.

## Validation plan for the business model

The value proposition canvas and Lean Canvas templates (`docs/templates/bmc.md`) are prepared for customer
interviews next semester (P5). Interviews: one operator, one maintenance technician and one EHS supervisor per plant
as a minimum. Hypotheses to test: (1) plants spend ≥ 1 h/day cleaning a machining area by hand; (2) they pay for
hazardous-waste disposal and would value segregation; (3) MXN 60,000 + 1,500/month is within a maintenance budget
without capital approval; (4) a pilot is required before purchase.
